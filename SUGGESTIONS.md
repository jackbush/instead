# Writing suggestions

How to write a line for `src/shared/suggestions.txt`. Each suggestion is the only thing on the page, set huge, so every word has to earn its place.

1. **Starts with a verb.** It also has to read well after "Instead,", because the website's hero builds that sentence from the file: "Instead, find a leaf and draw it."
2. **Doable now, with what's in reach.** No booking, gear or waiting for a day off.
3. **Free.** Nothing to buy.
4. **Specific and finite.** It has an end you'll recognise: one song, one chapter, round the block.
5. **Off the screen.** If it needs a device, it needs a reason: a call, a song, a question you want answered.
6. **Short.** Eight words and 40 characters at most, counting a link as its text. The type is set at up to 9rem in a 14-character measure.
7. **An invitation, not a correction.** No "stop", no "should", no productivity guilt.
8. **Sentence case, no full stop, British spelling.** Practise, colour, favourite.
9. **Short and direct.** Say the thing. No "why not", no "take a moment to", no "embrace", "journey", "mindful moment" or "nourish".

| Instead of | Write |
| --- | --- |
| Why not take a moment to enjoy some tea? | Drink a cup of tea in silence |
| Go to a yoga class | Have a stretch |
| Stop scrolling and read | Read a chapter of your book |
| Get some exercise | Walk around the block, the long way |
| Reply to the messages you've been ignoring | Tell a friend why you love them |

## Packs

Every suggestion sits in one pack, under its `# Name` heading. A new pack needs a one-word name, since it becomes a checkbox in the settings. Keep a pack to everyday things anyone could do; a niche interest is what Custom is for.

`npm test` checks the length, case and full stop of every line.
